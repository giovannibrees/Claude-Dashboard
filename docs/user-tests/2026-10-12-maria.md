# User test: Maria (68, IT basic, phone, poor eyesight, careful)
Date 2026-10-12 · 390px phone with browser zoom 200% (195px CSS width, 4x), touch · reads every screen first

Goals: answer her agents' questions, see whether anything needs her.

## Steps
1. Open page. Sees the big "3 Need you" tile in orange: she understands something needs her. But the header is broken: "Agent Hub" title overlaps the "Message all leads" button, "Add project" is cut off the right edge, "Live" text is crossed by the button. (img/maria-1-home-200.png)
2. Taps the "3 Need you" tile: page scrolls to "Needs you". The first card's colour table runs outside the card border and off screen: the "Hex" and "Use" columns can't be read without sideways scrolling, and the whole page scrolls sideways (scrollWidth 302 vs 195). (img/maria-2-needs-tap.png)
3. Scrolls to "Test cleanup" question, reads the 2 numbered questions, taps Approve. Card turns into "Approved just now. Not picked up yet." She does not know what "picked up" means or whether she is finished. (img/maria-3-question-card.png, maria-4-after-approve.png)

## Findings
- bug: at 200% zoom the header overlaps (title / "Message all leads" / "Add project"), main actions are partly off screen. Public CSS: `.top-row` has no wrap/stack for narrow widths (index page, `.top-row`, `.brand`).
- bug: colour table in approval cards is wider than the card at 200% (min-width 360px) and makes the page scroll sideways; tile text "Deliveries to review" also spills out of its tile.
- confusing: "Not picked up yet" under an answered card; "P1 project" chip; "Agent proceeds with its recommendation in 3h 53m" is long and alarming.
- low contrast: "P1 project" chip 4.42:1 and "Recommendation:" label 4.42:1 (orange on tint), below 4.5:1; chips are 13px.
- tap targets under 44px: filter selects 40px, Clear filters 37, "Message" links 37, agent name links 25, priority select 30, auto-release checkbox 13x13, profile selects 32. The 4 answer buttons are 45px (good).
No blocker: the answer went through.
